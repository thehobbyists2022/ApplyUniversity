StepOne College: Account & Data Deletion Request

At StepOne College (operated by Clarity Clinical Solutions LLC), we respect your privacy and provide a simple, transparent path to permanently delete your account and all associated application data.

Zero Server Tracking Guarantee: StepOne College operates under a local-first architecture. Your essay brainstorming drafts, activity lists, and college preference matrices are stored 100% locally on your device and are never sold or shared with third parties.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Option 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the StepOne College app on your Android device.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. In the top navigation header, click on your user profile chip / avatar.
  4. Click the red Delete Account (Trash icon) button.
  5. Confirm the prompt. Your user record in Supabase Authentication (auth.users) and all local device data will be permanently wiped immediately.

Option 2: Web / Email Request (Without App Installed)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your mobile device, you can submit a deletion request via email:

  • Send an email to: support@steponecareer.com
  • Subject: StepOne College Account Deletion Request
  • Body: Please provide the registered email address associated with your StepOne College account.
  • Processing Time: Requests submitted via email are verified and purged within 48 business hours. You will receive a confirmation email once complete.

What Data is Deleted

  • Authentication Identity: User ID, email address, password hash, and active session tokens.
  • Educational Data: All saved college lists, compare lists, and application notes.
  • Essay & Activity Drafts: Cleared from local storage and any cloud-synced cache.

Data Retention & Exceptions

No user educational data is retained following deletion. The only exception applies to billing transaction records (if you purchased a subscription via Stripe), which are securely retained by our payment processor (Stripe) solely to satisfy statutory legal, accounting, and IRS tax compliance requirements for the legally required period.
